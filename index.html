Frequently Asked Questions

Can you help launch a new e-commerce business?

Absolutely. We help businesses launch from the ground up, managing everything from account setup and store development to product listings, optimisation and ongoing management. We provide the expertise and support needed to launch your e-commerce business with confidence.

Do you work with established businesses?

Yes. We work with established businesses looking to increase sales, improve performance, expand into new marketplaces or outsource their day-to-day e-commerce operations. Whether you need support in specific areas or complete account management, we tailor our services to your business.

Which platforms do you manage?

We provide professional management across Amazon, Shopify, TikTok Shop, eBay, Temu and Noon. We also support advertising through Google Ads and Meta Ads, helping businesses manage and grow their online presence across multiple channels.

Do you offer fully managed e-commerce services?

Yes. We offer complete, hands-off e-commerce management, taking care of day-to-day operations, product listings, order processing, customer enquiries and ongoing optimisation. This allows you to focus on your other business interests and priorities while we manage your online operations.

How much do your services cost?

Our pricing depends on your business requirements, the platforms you operate on and the level of management you need. We offer flexible packages tailored to your business, whether you require specific services or complete e-commerce management. Any advertising spend is separate from our management fees and is paid directly by the client. Contact us for a personalised quotation.

Do you handle packing, storage or shipping?

We do not directly handle packing or shipping. However, we can oversee shipping operations and coordinate with fulfilment providers on your behalf. For businesses requiring storage solutions, we work with trusted partners who may be able to offer suitable warehousing and storage packages.

Do I retain ownership of my accounts?

Yes. You retain ownership, with management access arranged through agreed permissions.

Do I need to commit to a long-term contract?

We offer flexible management arrangements tailored to your business requirements. Contract terms and the scope of services are discussed and agreed before we begin, ensuring you understand exactly what's included.
